Sanita Personal AIOS

Sanita Personal AIOS is a private, personal tool used by one person, Sanita Pinchback, to stay on top of her own household.

What it does

  • Reads Sanita's own Gmail inbox and calendar, read-only. It cannot change, label, archive, or delete anything.
  • Sends Sanita one email each morning: a summary of what's due, what came in, and today's calendar. That email goes only to Sanita.

It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else can sign in to it.
