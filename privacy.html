Privacy Policy: Sanita Personal AIOS

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Sanita Personal AIOS is a personal tool used only by its owner, Sanita Pinchback.

What it accesses. With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, it reads her own Gmail messages and calendar (read-only) and sends one daily summary email to her own address. It requests no other Google data.

How the data is used. Only to build the owner's daily summary. Short excerpts of some emails may be summarized by an AI service on a paid plan that does not use the data for training. Emails from health care providers, insurers, and attorneys are never sent to an AI service.

Storage. Summaries are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Google user data. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact. Use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
